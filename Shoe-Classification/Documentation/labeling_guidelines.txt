SHOE BRAND CLASSIFICATION - LABELING GUIDELINES

OBJECTIVE: Identify the brand of footwear in each image

LABEL DEFINITIONS:
- Adidas: Any shoe displaying Adidas logo (three stripes) or branding
- Nike: Any shoe displaying Nike swoosh or "NIKE" text
- Converse: Any shoe displaying Converse star logo or "CONVERSE" text
- Other: Shoes with no visible branding or non-target brands

EDGE CASES:
- Multiple shoes in frame → Label the most prominent/centered shoe
- Unclear/blurry branding → Use "Other"
- Mixed brand elements → Label by logo presence, not design similarity

QUALITY STANDARDS:
- Minimum 90% consistency required
- Review at least 20% of labels for accuracy
- Flag ambiguous images for secondary review
